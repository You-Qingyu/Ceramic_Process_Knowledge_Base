About This Site

The Ceramic Craft Knowledge Base is a public, free-of-charge encyclopedia of ceramic craftsmanship, openly compiled from publicly available materials and free for everyone to use. Its knowledge system spans the complete workflow — raw materials such as clay, quartz and feldspar; forming by throwing, slip casting and pressing; glazing and decoration in underglaze blue and monochrome glazes; firing schedules and kiln types; as well as famous kilns and wares, traditional crafts, common defects and advanced ceramics. Categories are precisely structured so you can narrow down quickly, and fast full-text search matches titles, summaries, tags and body text the moment you type. Register to contribute entries — published after an admin review and credited to you — join the forum discussions, or ask the AI assistant in the bottom-right corner. All resources are open and free to use, share and reference.
